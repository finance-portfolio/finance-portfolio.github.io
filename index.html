The short version

A little more about me.

Every week I compile the bookings, ARR and quota-attainment reporting that reaches the executive team, the board and a private-equity sponsor — pulling from four systems that rarely agree and working out why before anyone has to ask.

Those systems disagree because each one recognises a deal at a different moment. A large part of the job is finding where a number sits, why two sources differ, and explaining the movement in one line a non-finance reader can act on.

I don't set the forecasts; the field and finance leadership do. What I own is making them reportable — the right version, the explained variance, and a number that survives scrutiny. What I want next is a senior seat where that judgement is used earlier, closer to the decision.
